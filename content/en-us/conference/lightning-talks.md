## Lightning Talk Rules

* **Lightning Talk topics** should be related to **technology** or **tech communities**, with **Python**-related topics especially welcome!
* All Lightning Talk content **must comply with the [:prose-locale-link{href="/about/code-of-conduct"}[PyCon Taiwan Code of Conduct]]** and avoid content that may cause discomfort to the audience.
* Each participant is limited to one talk. **Only on-site participants may present**; remote presentations are not accepted.
* Each selected speaker will have **3 minutes** to present. The talk will be stopped when time is up.
* **Selection and Notification:** At 14:00 daily, **selected applicants** will be notified via email and Discord to confirm their participation by 14:30. If no response is received within the time limit, the slot will be filled by an alternate.

## How to Register

**Lightning Talk registration is on the day of the event.**

1. **How to Register:** Please scan the QR code at the venue and fill out the Google Form to register.
2. **Form Details:** You will need to provide your **nickname**, **contact information**, and **Lightning Talk topic**.
3. **Registration Time:** **08:00 - 13:00** daily (late sign-ups will not be accepted)
4. **Results:** Selected applicants will be notified via email and Discord at **14:00** each day. Please confirm your participation by **14:30**.

## FAQ

**What are Lightning Talks?** 

Lightning Talks are fast-paced, 3-minute talks where anyone can share an idea, project, or experience with the PyCon Taiwan community. Whether you’re a seasoned developer or a newcomer, if you have something you’re passionate about sharing, you’re welcome to join!

**What can I share?**

* A recent project or discovery
* Learning insights and practical tips
* A technical insight you can share in 3 minutes
* Any interesting Python-related topics

**Is my topic too simple?**

No! Your unique perspective can offer something new to people from different backgrounds.

**Can I share if I'm not an expert?**

Absolutely! As long as you're passionate about a topic, learning insights or initial findings are worth sharing.

**What if I get nervous speaking?**

That's perfectly normal! The PyCon Taiwan community is incredibly friendly, and the sense of accomplishment after presenting will make it all worthwhile.
