## Collect Stickers, Get a Raffle Ticket, and Win Prizes!

Visiting booths is a great way to meet community partners and discover new technologies—and you may even win a prize! Grab your attendee badge and start collecting stickers! ✨

## 🎯 How to Play

1. **Find the Game Card**｜The Booth Game card is printed on the back of your attendee badge.
2. **Visit the Booths**｜Stop by the designated booths and chat with our partners to collect their stickers.
3. **Collect All Stickers**｜Fill all 12 spaces on your game card.
4. **Get a Raffle Ticket**｜Show your completed card and follow the staff’s instructions to receive a raffle ticket.
5. **Enter the Draw**｜Fill in your details and drop the ticket into the raffle box for a chance to win!

## 🎁 Prizes

* PyCon Everywhere × 1
* Trend Micro Antivirus Software × 3
* Selected Books

## 📌 Notes

**Raffle tickets and prizes are limited and available on a first-come, first-served basis. Please follow on-site announcements for the final activity and prize redemption details.**

**Get your attendee badge ready, visit the booths, and start collecting stickers! 🚀**
